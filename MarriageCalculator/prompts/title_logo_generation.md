# Title Logo Generation Prompt

**Objective:** Create a premium title logo for the "Marriage Calculator" mobile app.

**Platform:** ChatGPT (DALL-E 3) or Midjourney

---

### Prompt Text:

> I need a premium title logo for my mobile card game app, "Marriage Calculator".
>
> **Design Requirements:**
> 1.  **Text:** "Marriage Calculator" (stacked on two lines).
> 2.  **Style:** Elegant, glossy 3D gold serif typography. It should look luxurious and high-end, fitting a casino or festival theme.
> 3.  **Color Context:** The app background is a deep dark maroon (#2D0A0A). The gold text needs to have the right lighting and shadow to "pop" clearly against this dark background.
> 4.  **Format:** Please generate this as a **PNG with a transparent background**. Do NOT include a solid color background, and do NOT include a "checkerboard" pattern. Just the isolated 3D text floating on transparency.
