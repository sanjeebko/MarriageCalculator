# Design Prompts for Marriage Game Calculator (Dashain & Tihar Theme)

Use these prompts in your AI design tool to generate UI mockups. The theme is **Dashain & Tihar Festival** (Nepal's biggest festivals), characterized by vibrant red, gold, marigold yellow, and warm lighting.

## Global Style
**Aesthetic**: Premium, Festive, Culturally Rich, Warm.
**Color Palette**:
*   Primary: Deep Red (Tika color) and Marigold Orange.
*   Background: Deep Night Blue (Tihar night) or Rich Maroon.
*   Accents: Gold (Wealth/Lakshmi), Fresh Green (Jamara).
**Motifs**: Marigold garlands (Sayapatri), Clay Lamps (Diyo), Rangoli patterns, Kites (Changa), Bamboo Swings (Ping), Playing Cards.
**Typography**: Clean but with a touch of elegance (serif headings for "Royal/Festival" feel, sans-serif for numbers).

### 2. Splash Screen
**Prompt**:
> Premium Splash Screen for "Marriage Calculator". Theme: Tihar (Festival of Lights).
> *   **Background**: Deep purple/blue gradient representing a festival night.
> *   **Foreground**: A glowing, intricate **Rangoli** floor design made of flower petals in the center.
> *   **Logo**: The "Marriage Calculator" text floating above the Rangoli in Metallic Gold 3D letters.
> *   **Decorations**: Softly glowing clay lamps (Diyos) placed around the bottom edges. A string of Marigold flowers hanging from the top.
> *   **Vibe**: Warm, inviting, celebratory, and wealthy.

### 3. Login & Auth Screen

> *   **Background**: Dark Maroon with a subtle watermark pattern of "Jamara" (barley grass) and "Kites".
> *   **Card**: A central frosted glass card for the login form.

### 4. Dashboard (Home)
**Prompt**:
> Festive App Dashboard.
> *   **Header**: A header background featuring a "Dhaka Topi" fabric pattern or rich red fabric texture. User avatar has a circular gold border.
> *   **Greeting**: "Happy Vijaya Dashami, Sanjeeb!".
> *   **Main Action ("New Game")**: A large card with a background of floating **Kites** in a blue sky. The button is Red with Gold text.
> *   **Secondary Action ("Join")**: A card featuring a **Diyo** (Oil Lamp) icon, symbolizing joining the light/circle.
> *   **Stats**: Recent games list. Winners are highlighted with a "Tika" icon or Green Jamara color. Losers have a neutral gray tone.

### 5. Game Setup (Lobby)
**Prompt**:
> Game Setup Screen with "Mandala" seating.
> *   **Seating Arrangement**: The 5-player seating is visualized not just as a table, but as a circular **Rangoli** or **Mandala** on the floor.
> *   **Seats**: Each player's position is marked by a small cushion (Chakaati) or a plate (Thaal).
> *   **Controls**:
>     *   Currency Selector: Gold dropdown.
>     *   Game Mode (Kidnap/Murder/Normal): Styled like traditional wooden toggles or scroll buttons.
> *   **Theme**: The background has hanging lights (Mala) visible at the top.

### 6. Round Input (Main Calculator)

### 7. Scoreboard & Settlement
**Prompt**:
> Financial Scoreboard, high contrast.
> *   **Total Balance**: Displayed on a background that looks like a new currency note or a Red Ledger Book (Bahi Khata).
> *   **Positive Scores**: Displayed in Bright Green (Jamara color) or Gold.
> *   **Negative Scores**: Displayed in Deep Red.
> *   **Settlement Button**: "Settle Accounts" button features an icon of a "Kalash" (Holy water pot) or just a handshake functionality.
